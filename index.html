Who I am

ABOUT ME

I'm Neel Patel — a 2nd year Diploma in Mechatronics student at K.H. Kabbur Institute of Engineering, and a competitive gamer who has been editing since 2020 — six years of turning raw gameplay into content.

On the server I play Valorant, Dota 2, CS 2, BGMI and Free Fire. Playing competitively taught me what a highlight actually feels like — which is exactly what makes an edit land.

Off the server I mainly work in CapCut Pro, with DaVinci Resolve and Premiere alongside it — gaming montages, vertical reels and short-form cuts. Thumbnails and posters I build with AI tools, which I've got genuinely good at steering. Engineering discipline, gamer instincts, editor's eye.
