01 Who I am

ABOUT ME

I'm Neel Patel, a 2nd year Mechatronics Engineering student based in Dharwad. My course sits right where electronics, mechanics and software meet — CAD, embedded systems, robotics and mechanism design — and that's the work I want to build a career in.

Alongside the diploma I've been editing video for 2+ years. It started as a hobby cutting my own gameplay and turned into real deliverables: promotional and recap videos for school and college events, cut to a deadline. I work mainly in CapCut Pro, with DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro alongside it, and I build thumbnails and posters using AI tools, which I've got genuinely good at steering.

I also play competitively — Valorant, CS 2, Dota 2, BGMI and Free Fire — and I IGL for my BGMI squad. It's where the shot-calling and the sense of timing come from, and it's the reason my edits land where they should.

Engineering discipline, an editor's eye, and a habit of finishing what I start.
